Problem Statement

The Speech Disconnect: 74 Million Excluded

02 / 12
Clinical Etiology
  • Dysarthria is motor neuromotor speech impairment: intact cognitive linguistic centers, but impaired neuromotor vocal tract execution.
  • Prevalent across Cerebral Palsy, ALS, Parkinson's, and Stroke survivors.
Failure of Mainstream Voice AI
  • Commercial models (Siri, Alexa, Google STT) yield 78% – 85% Word Error Rate.
  • Acoustic variance (prolonged vowels, hypernasality, imprecise consonants) causes traditional acoustic models to fail entirely.
Theoretical Motivation

Why Heuristics Fail & Transformers Generalize

03 / 12
Limitations of Rule-Based / Acoustic Heuristics
  • Extreme Inter-Speaker Heterogeneity: Dysarthric articulation patterns vary unpredictably between speakers with identical clinical diagnoses.
  • Non-Linear Temporal Distortion: Speech rate fluctuates wildly within single sentences; fixed-window phonetic heuristics break down.
  • Inability to Leverage Context: Rule systems cannot use contextual language probability to resolve ambiguous phonetic inputs.
Advantages of Transformer ASR (Whisper)
  • Massive Prior Knowledge: Pre-trained on 680,000 hours of diverse acoustic signals to capture robust latent phonetic spaces.
  • Multi-Head Cross-Attention: The decoder dynamically attends across the entire 30s encoder spectrogram representation to disambiguate blurred phonemes.
  • Joint Acoustic & Language Modeling: Autoregressive decoding seamlessly applies language priors to reconstruct intended vocabulary.

Partitioning & SpecAugment Defense
  • Speaker-Independent Split (80/10/10): Partitioning is strictly enforced by subject identity, guaranteeing test sets evaluate true zero-shot speaker generalizability.
  • Temporal Perturbation: Audio speed scaled between 0.85× and 1.15× to simulate erratic speaking rates.
  • Spectral Masking: Random block zeroing in time/frequency bins (SpecAugment) to prevent overfitting on limited speaker vocal tract resonances.

Implementation Details

Acoustic Preprocessing to Token Generation

07 / 12
1. Signal & Spectrogram

Audio streams are converted into 16kHz single-channel floating arrays. A 25ms Hann window with 10ms shift extracts 80-channel log-magnitude Mel-spectrograms.

Input: [1 × 480,000]
Output: [80 × 3000] Matrix
2. Transformer Encoding

Two 1D convolutional layers downsample the spectrogram by 2×. Sinusoidal positional embeddings are added before 6 layers of self-attention extract 512-dim acoustic representations.

Downsampling: Conv1D (Stride 2)
Latent Repr: [1500 × 512]
3. Autoregressive Decode

Decoder uses learned byte-pair token embeddings, masked causal self-attention, and cross-attention over encoder states. Employs Beam Search (beam_size=5) with VAD suppression.

Vocabulary: 51,865 Tokens
Output: Timestamps & Confidence
Training Methodology

Two-Stage Transfer Learning & Anti-Forgetting

08 / 12
Stage 1: Frozen Encoder Adaptation (Epochs 1–10)

Encoder weights are locked. Only decoder attention matrices are optimized to prevent catastrophic forgetting of base acoustic phoneme spaces.

Stage 2: Full End-to-End Fine-Tuning (Epochs 11–15)

Entire network is unfreezed with a conservative learning rate (η = 1e-5, AdamW optimizer, cosine annealing schedule), tailoring low-level convolutional filters to dysarthric resonance.

Clinical Interpretation
  • Why 38.6% WER is Transformative: In severe motor dysarthria, human caretakers achieve ~22% WER relying on contextual intuition. VoiceAid provides sufficient lexical anchoring for families to understand utterances without repetition.

  • No External FFmpeg Binaries: Custom client-side Float32 PCM encoder sends native 16kHz WAV, eliminating OS-level dependency vulnerabilities.
