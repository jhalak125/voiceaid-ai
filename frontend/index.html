About This Project

🎯

Problem

Dysarthria affects ~74 million people globally. Standard voice assistants like Siri and Alexa fail on dysarthric speech, leaving patients isolated from voice-based technology.

💡

Solution

Fine-tuning OpenAI Whisper on the TORGO dysarthric speech dataset, reducing Word Error Rate from ~80% to under 40% for speech-impaired users.

📈

Impact

Enables independent communication for Cerebral Palsy, ALS, Parkinson's, and stroke patients — restoring voice to those who need it most.

  • University of Toronto

🗃️ UASpeech Dataset

  • 765 words × 155 speakers
  • Cerebral Palsy patients
  • 4 intelligibility levels
  • University of Illinois
